# How to Make an IndexStep Guide

**Category:** DIY  
**Difficulty:** 2 – Medium  
**Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cover Image:** (upload a screenshot of the IndexStep create page or the orange folder logo)

---

## Overview

IndexStep lets you create step-by-step visual guides for anything — DIY projects, recipes, tech tutorials, crafts, and more. This tutorial walks you through the full process of creating and publishing your first guide.

---

## Step 1: Sign In

Open [indexstep](/) and click **Sign Up** if you don't have an account yet, or **Log In** if you do.

Once signed in, click your profile icon in the top-right corner to access your dashboard.

---

## Step 2: Start a New Guide

Click the **Create Guide** button in the navbar or on the homepage.

You'll land on the Create Guide page with three main sections:

- **Basic Information** — title, description, category, difficulty, time
- **Ingredients & Materials** — tools, ingredients, supplies (category-specific)
- **Steps** — the actual step-by-step instructions with photos

---

## Step 3: Fill In Basic Information

**Title** — Give your guide a clear, descriptive name. E.g. *"How to Build a Wooden Birdhouse"* or *"Fix a Squeaky Door Hinge."*

**Description** — Write 1-3 sentences explaining what the user will learn or accomplish.

**Category** — Choose the category that best fits your guide:
- DIY
- Cooking
- Tech & Computers
- Crafts
- Gaming
- Music
- Fitness
- Photography
- Other

**Difficulty** — Rate it 1–5:
1. Easy — anyone can do it
2. Medium — some experience helpful
3. Hard — requires skill or special tools
4. Expert — for experienced practitioners
5. Master — professional-level

**Time (minutes)** — Estimate how long the project takes from start to finish.

**Cover Image** — Upload a wide image (16:9 ratio works best) that represents your guide. This shows up in search results and on the guide card.

---

## Step 4: Add Ingredients & Materials

The **Ingredients & Materials** section adapts to your chosen category:

- **Cooking** → Ingredients (amount, unit, notes)
- **DIY / Crafts** → Materials (quantity, size, kind, notes)
- **Tech** → Tools & Software (name, version, notes)
- **Gaming** → Requirements (platform, edition, notes)
- And so on for each category

Click **Add Item** and select the category tab to add items. Fill in:
- **Name** — what the item is
- **Quantity** — how many you need (cooking: cups, tbsp, etc.)
- **Size / Type** — optional details (e.g., "2x4 lumber", "8-inch pan")
- **Notes** — any special considerations

You can also click **Customize Fields** to create your own category with custom field labels.

---

## Step 5: Add Steps

Click **Add Step** to create your first step. Each step has:

- **Step Title** — a short name for this step (e.g., "Cut the wood")
- **Photo** — drag & drop or click to upload an image showing what this step looks like
- **Instructions** — describe the step in detail

Add as many steps as needed. You can reorder them later (drag handle on the left), and delete steps with the trash icon.

**Tips for good steps:**
- One clear action per step
- Include a photo whenever possible
- Be specific about measurements, positions, and expectations
- If something is tricky, mention it in the notes

---

## Step 6: Choose Access — Free or Paid?

In the **Basic Information** section, toggle **Guide Access**:

- **Free** — anyone can view the full guide
- **Locked** — only people who pay can see the content. Set a price in dollars.

If you enable **"Lock content, show title"**, visitors will see your guide title and description but need to pay to see the steps and materials.

---

## Step 7: Publish or Save as Draft

When you're done:

- Click **Save Draft** to keep it unpublished while you finish later
- Click **Publish Guide** to make it live and visible to everyone

Once published, your guide appears in search results and on the homepage.

---

## Tips for a Great Guide

- **Cover image matters** — a good thumbnail gets more clicks
- **Photos per step** — show, don't just tell
- **Be specific** — "sand the wood smooth" is better than "sand the wood"
- **Start simple** — step 1 should be accessible to your target difficulty level
- **Test it yourself** — follow your own guide before publishing

---

## Editing & Deleting

Visit your guide and click **Edit** to make changes. Admins and moderators can also edit any guide on the platform.

---

*Happy building!*
